## Prompt do primeiro atlas

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one production pixel-art portrait atlas for a Brazilian satirical SDL2 arcade fighting game. Reference image is STYLE ONLY, preserve its chunky visible square pixels, limited 16-bit shading, strongly recognizable faces, inked anime-like expressions but not photorealism. New atlas MUST have exactly 2 columns and 2 rows of equal square tiles filling the entire square canvas, no gutters, no text, no labels. Tile order row-major: top-left JAIR BOLSONARO, older angular face, gray-brown side parted hair, clean shaven, navy suit green yellow tie, green pixel background; top-right DANIEL VORCARO, businessman with full dark beard, short dark side-parted hair, broad face, dark suit, golden ochre pixel background; bottom-left KIM KATAGUIRI, young Japanese-Brazilian man, straight black hair, rectangular black glasses, clean shaven, dark suit, blue pixel background; bottom-right ARTHUR DO VAL, young Brazilian man short dark hair and dark beard, slightly long face, black casual jacket, orange pixel background. Each is a head-and-shoulders portrait at same scale, face centered entirely within its own tile, looking slightly to the right, full hair visible. Strong caricature likeness, bold brows, hard pixel clusters, no smooth gradients. Pixels visibly square like the supplied reference. This is an asset for a fictional satirical fighting game.
